About ATP Geopolitics Map

The ATP Geopolitics Map is a bespoke, interactive geospatial intelligence platform designed for high-fidelity mapping data and global geopolitical analysis, with a heavy focus on the conflict in Ukraine. It offers open-source, zero-cost tracking of front lines, satellite imagery, thermal data, and more. This map powers the visuals on the ATP Geopolitics YouTube channel.
